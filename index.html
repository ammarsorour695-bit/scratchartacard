The slider under the board and the tip buttons here both change the same size. No toothpick removes the toothpick so you scratch with just the cursor. No music, just scratching sounds.

How to play

  • 1 Pick a level from the menu. Use the arrows or the bottom bar to browse.
  • 2 Hold and drag the toothpick over the blackboard to scratch it away.
  • 3 Only the main drawing is colorable. The border flowers and stars are decoration and stay uncolorable.
  • 4 Use the tip size slider under the board: small for tiny parts, big to fill fast.
  • 5 The rainbow bar at the top shows how much you revealed. No pop-ups — scratch as much as you like.
Use the speaker icon to mute the scratch sound, and the gear icon for tip presets, chalk guide, no toothpick and toothpick tilt.
